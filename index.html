What hunters say

Questions

When is duck season in Stuttgart, Arkansas?

Arkansas's 2026-27 duck, coot and merganser season runs in three splits: Nov. 21-29, Dec. 10-23, and Dec. 26, 2026 through Jan. 31, 2027, per the Arkansas Game and Fish Commission. Specklebelly and snow goose seasons open Oct. 31-Nov. 8 and then run with the duck splits; early Canada goose is Sept. 1-Oct. 15. Call Jonathon Goins at 870-830-3633 to book your dates.

How do I book a guided duck hunt in Stuttgart, Arkansas?

Call or text Jonathon Goins directly at 870-830-3633. We discuss dates, group size, and what you want to hunt. Book early. Peak Stuttgart duck season dates fill up fast, and we keep group sizes small for a better hunt experience.

What duck and goose species can I hunt in Stuttgart, Arkansas?

Stuttgart sits at the heart of the Mississippi Flyway on the Arkansas Grand Prairie. Duck species include mallard, pintail, gadwall, widgeon, teal, canvasback, redhead, wood duck, black duck, and ringneck. Goose species include specklebelly (white-fronted goose), Canada goose, and snow goose. Daily limit is 6 ducks. Verify current limits at agfc.com.

What license do I need to hunt in Arkansas?

All hunters are required to have a valid Arkansas hunting license. Purchase online at agfc.com or by calling 800-364-4263. Contact Jonathon Goins at 870-830-3633 with any questions before your hunt.

What should I bring on a Stuttgart duck hunt?

Bring your shotgun (12-gauge recommended), steel shot shells only, no lead (3-inch or 3.5-inch, #2 through #4 shot), your Arkansas hunting license, chest waders, and warm waterproof camo clothing. We provide decoys, blinds, and calling.

Is lodging available for out-of-town hunters?

Yes, lodging is available for an additional fee for hunters traveling to Stuttgart, Arkansas. Call 870-830-3633 for current availability and rates. We can comfortably accommodate groups for multi-day hunts.

How many hunters are allowed per group?

We keep group sizes at 4 or more hunters. Small groups mean better blind setups, tighter spreads, and a more personal guided hunt experience. For private groups of up to 6, ask about the Jonboy's Duck Club private blind membership.

What types of duck hunts does The Roost Hole Guide Service offer?

The Roost Hole Guide Service offers three types of guided waterfowl hunts in Stuttgart, Arkansas: flooded rice field duck hunting on the Arkansas Grand Prairie, field spread hunts for ducks and geese over harvested grain fields, and layout hunts for ducks and Canada geese in dry agricultural fields. All hunts are on the Mississippi Flyway.
