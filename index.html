About Your Guide

Jonathon Goins.
Life-Long Waterfowler.

I'm Jonathon Goins, and waterfowl hunting is what I do. Not as a hobby — as a way of life. For over 15 years, I've been in the timber before sunrise, setting decoys in frozen fields, and reading the weather like it's the only language that matters.

The Roost Hole isn't a corporate operation. It's me, the land I know around Stuttgart, and the birds that work it. I don't promise limits. I promise honest hunting — the kind where you earn it, respect it, and remember it.

We go where the birds go, and we do it right: small groups, proven setups, and no shortcuts. If you want a real hunt with a guide who lives this life year-round, let's talk.

What We Offer

Hunt
Offerings

We hunt the way birds want to be hunted. That means reading conditions daily, moving when we need to, and never settling for a second-rate setup. Every hunt type below has its own season and its own art.

01 🌾

Flooded Rice Fields

Stuttgart's legendary rice field hunting. Mallards, pintails, and gadwall working flooded crops. Shallow water, big decoy spreads, and the sight of birds dropping feet-down at first light. This is what Arkansas waterfowling is all about.

02 🪿

Field Spreads

Ducks and geese over large decoy spreads in harvested grain fields. We set up where they want to be — corn, rice, soybeans — and let them come. Timing and concealment are everything here, and we've got both dialed in.

03 🎯

Layout Hunts

Low-profile field hunting for ducks and Canada geese. Get flat, stay still, and watch them lock up over the spread. There's nothing like the moment a big flock of geese commits from 500 yards out. Pure, unfiltered waterfowling.

Got Questions

Before
You Book

Everything you need to know before your hunt. Still have questions? Call or text Jonathon directly.

870-830-3633
What should I bring on the hunt? +

Bring your shotgun, shells (steel shot required), valid Arkansas hunting license, warm waterproof clothing, and waders. We provide decoys, blinds, and calling. When in doubt, call ahead and we'll walk you through everything.

Do you offer morning and afternoon hunts? +

Most hunts are morning hunts when duck activity is highest. We start well before sunrise and make the most of those prime early hours. Afternoon hunts may be available depending on conditions and season.

How do I book a hunt? +

Call or text 870-830-3633 to check availability. We'll discuss dates, group size, and hunt preferences. Book early — peak season fills up fast and we keep group sizes small by design.

What's the cancellation policy? +

Weather happens. If conditions are unsafe or truly unfavorable for hunting, we'll work with you to reschedule. We're hunters too — we understand the sport and will always be straight with you.

Is lodging included in the hunt price? +

Lodging is available for an additional fee. Call 870-830-3633 for current availability and rates. We can accommodate multiple hunters comfortably — perfect for groups traveling from out of state.

How many hunters per group? +

We keep group sizes at 4 or more hunters. Small groups mean better setups, tighter blinds, and a more personal hunt. This isn't a crowd experience — it's a real guided hunt.
