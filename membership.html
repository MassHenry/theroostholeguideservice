About the Club

Where the serious hunters come.

Jonboy's Duck Club isn't a booking service. It's a membership — a private circle of hunters who want more than a one-off trip.

Stuttgart, Arkansas is the Duck Capital of the World for a reason. The flooded rice fields, the flyways, the migration routes — it's not hype. Jonathon has been hunting and guiding these grounds for over 15 years. He knows where the birds are before the birds do.

A club membership means you're locking in your access to those grounds for the season. You're not competing for dates. You're not sharing a blind with strangers. You're showing up to your blind, with your people, on your schedule.

Spots are limited. That's by design.
