Jonboy's Duck Club operates out of Stuttgart, Arkansas, the self-proclaimed Duck Capital of the World. Stuttgart sits in the heart of the Arkansas Grand Prairie, roughly 50 miles southeast of Little Rock, and is surrounded by some of the most productive flooded rice fields and waterfowl habitat in North America. The club hunts private land in and around the Stuttgart area, including flooded field spreads and layout setups that put you in the middle of the Mississippi Flyway migration.

Both membership tiers include full guided access to private duck hunting grounds in Stuttgart, Arkansas for the 2026-27 season. Individual members get shared blind access with small groups throughout the season. Full blind members get exclusive access to a private blind for up to 6 hunters, meaning no strangers, no shared hunts — just your group on your schedule. All hunts are guided by Jonathon Goins.

Stuttgart and the surrounding Grand Prairie are home to one of the greatest concentrations of migrating waterfowl in North America each winter. Members can expect mallards, pintail, gadwall, green-wing and blue-wing teal, widgeon, wood ducks, canvasback, redhead, and specklebelly geese throughout the season. The exact mix depends on conditions, but Stuttgart rarely disappoints — it sits where two major flyways converge near Bayou Meto and the White River National Wildlife Refuge.

Private Duck Hunting Club Membership in Stuttgart, Arkansas

Jonboy's Duck Club is a private waterfowl membership club located in Stuttgart, Arkansas, the Duck Capital of the World. The club offers limited seasonal memberships for the 2026-2027 Arkansas duck hunting season, providing access to privately leased flooded rice fields and field spreads on the Mississippi Flyway. Led by guide Jonathon Goins with over 15 years of experience hunting the Grand Prairie, the club provides small-group guided waterfowl hunts targeting mallard, pintail, gadwall, teal, widgeon, canvasback, and more. Stuttgart, Arkansas is situated in Arkansas County, at the intersection of two major flyways, making it one of the premier duck hunting destinations in the United States.

Membership options include an individual duck hunting membership at $2,500 per season and a full private blind duck hunting membership at $8,500 per season for up to six hunters. Private blind memberships are ideal for corporate duck hunting groups, family hunts, and friend groups looking for exclusive waterfowl access in Stuttgart, AR. The club is affiliated with The Roost Hole Guide Service. For inquiries about duck hunting club membership in Stuttgart, Arkansas, contact Jonathon Goins at 870-830-3633.
