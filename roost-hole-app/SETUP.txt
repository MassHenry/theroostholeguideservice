THE ROOST HOLE APP: GOING LIVE
About 30 minutes, one time. Free. Your Firebase project can't charge you on the
free plan, and no credit card is needed.

Until step 3 is done the app runs in practice mode, and changes stay on one phone.
Firebase moves its menus around now and then. If a name below doesn't match,
look for the closest one.


STEP 1. CREATE THE SHARED STORAGE (Firebase, from Google)
This is what lets a blind you assign on your phone show up on a member's phone.

  a. Go to console.firebase.google.com and sign in with a Google account.
  b. Click "Add project" or "Create a project". Name it roost-hole.
     Google Analytics and Gemini can be off.
  c. Left menu: Databases & Storage > Firestore. Click "Create database".
     If it asks for an edition, pick Standard. Pick a United States location.
     Choose "production mode". Click Create.
  d. Left menu: Security > Authentication. Click "Get started" if you see it.
     On the Sign-in method tab, turn on "Email/Password" and save.
     On the Users tab, click "Add user" one time. This one account is for all owners:
       Email: owner@theroostholeguideservice.com  (no mail is ever sent to it)
       Password: your owner code. This is what your people will type. Use at least 6
       lowercase letters and numbers, no spaces, and make it hard to guess.
  e. Then, on the Settings tab under User actions, turn off "Enable create (sign-up)"
     if you see it. Nobody else needs an account.


STEP 2. PASTE THE SECURITY RULES
  a. Open firestore.rules from this download in any text editor (Notepad works).
     It's already filled in. Don't change anything.
  b. In Firebase: Firestore > Rules tab. Replace everything there with the
     contents of the file. Click Publish.


STEP 3. CONNECT THE APP
  a. In Firebase, click the gear icon > Project settings.
  b. Under "Your apps", click the web icon </>. Register it with any name.
     Skip Firebase Hosting.
  c. It shows a block of code. Find the values for apiKey and projectId.
  d. Open app/config.js from this download and paste those two values between
     the quotes. Save. (This key is meant to be public. The rules protect the data.)


STEP 4. PUT IT ON YOUR WEBSITE
  a. Upload the whole "app" folder to your website, next to your images folder,
     so this address opens the app:
     https://theroostholeguideservice.com/app/
     If your web host won't take an uploaded folder, the app can live on Firebase
     Hosting for free instead. Your site just links to it.
  b. Add a button on your site that links to it. For example:
     <a href="/app/">Get the App</a>
  c. get-the-app-qr.png is a QR code to the same address. Print it for the lodge,
     the truck, or a flyer.
  d. Your site has its own date calendars ("Pick your dates" and "Check availability").
     They don't read from the app. Point those buttons at /app/ so you only have
     one calendar to keep up.


STEP 5. SET IT UP BEFORE YOU SHARE THE LINK
  a. Open the app. Tap Duck Club, then "Owner sign in" at the bottom, and type the owner
     code. Each owner does this once on their own phone. There's no email or password
     to type, and the phone remembers it.
  b. Hunts tab: "Change my usual day" sets the hunts on every legal day.
     It starts with a morning duck hunt and a morning goose hunt. Set your real
     schedule first, because customers see this as soon as the app is live.
  c. Tap any day to mark one hunt booked, or to add, change, or remove hunts on
     that day. Removing every hunt closes the day.
  d. Duck Club > Members: paste your names, one per line.
  e. Duck Club > Blinds: tap + on a blind and tap names. Tap a name to move it.
     Use "Assigning for" to change one day without touching the season blinds.
  f. Check it on a second phone with no sign-in: book a hunt on yours and make
     sure it shows as booked on the other.
  g. Duck Club > Members: "Copy a text to send members" gives you the link and code.

On an iPhone the home-screen app and Safari don't share anything, so add the app to
your home screen first, then sign in there.


HOW PEOPLE GET IT
They open the link and add it to their home screen. No app store, account, or email.
  - iPhone: Safari's Share menu, then Add to Home Screen. The app walks them through it.
  - Android: the app shows an Install button, or it's in the browser's menu.
  - Facebook's built-in browser can't install apps. The app tells them to open it in
    their regular browser first.


GOOD TO KNOW
  - Anyone with the club code can see the blind list and names. If a member leaves,
    use "Change the code" and send out the new one.
  - Use first name and last initial if you want less on there.
  - Owners can work at the same time. Their changes combine, even on the same day.
    Only if two owners change the very same thing does the later save win, such as
    both moving the same hunter or one booking a hunt the other just reopened.
  - The owner code and the club code are different. The owner code lets someone change
    things, so only give it to owners. Members only get the club code.
  - Adding an owner: just give them the owner code.
  - Removing an owner, or if the code gets out: change the owner code. In Firebase, go
    to Authentication > Users, click the three dots on the account, and delete it. Then
    add it again with the same email and a new code. If Firebase won't let you add it,
    turn sign-up back on for a moment (step 1e). Phones using the old code get signed
    out within about an hour, and every owner types the new code once.
  - Same-day hunts drop off the customer calendar once they'd have started: morning
    at 5 AM, midday at 10 AM, afternoon at 1 PM. You still see them.
  - Bad signal won't lose your changes. The phone holds them and sends them when it
    can, and tells you until it has.
  - After you upload a new version, phones pick it up the second time they open the app.
  - Next season: change the dates at the bottom of app/config.js and upload that file.
  - Free plan limit: 50,000 reads a day, far more than this app will use. If it were
    ever hit, the data pauses until the limit resets. You're never billed.


TROUBLE?
  - "Practice mode" still showing: config.js wasn't saved or uploaded, or a quote or
    comma got removed from it.
  - "The code worked, but the app can't open the club data": the rules weren't published,
    or the account's email doesn't exactly match step 1d.
  - "That code didn't work": check the account on the Users tab from step 1d. The code
    has to be all lowercase there. If you can't remember it, change it the way shown
    under Good to Know.
